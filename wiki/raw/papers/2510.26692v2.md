# Kimi Linear: An Expressive, Efficient Attention Architecture

**Authors:** Kimi Team
**Affiliation:** Moonshot AI
**Venue:** Technical Report, arXiv preprint, Nov 2025
**arXiv:** 2510.26692

## Summary

Introduces Kimi Linear, a hybrid linear-attention architecture that — for the first time — outperforms full attention under fair comparisons across short-context, long-context, and RL scaling regimes. At its core is Kimi Delta Attention (KDA), an expressive linear attention module extending Gated DeltaNet (GDN) with a finer-grained, channel-wise gating mechanism. Pretrained at 3B activated / 48B total parameters, Kimi Linear matches or beats full Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA) with the same training recipe while cutting KV cache usage by up to 75% and achieving up to 6× decoding throughput at 1M context.

## Kimi Delta Attention (KDA)

- Extends the classical delta rule (DeltaNet): `S_t = S_{t-1} - β_t∇_S L_t(S_{t-1}) = (I - β_t k_t k_t^T)S_{t-1} + β_t k_t v_t^T`, an online-gradient-descent view of linear attention as associative memory.
- Gated DeltaNet (GDN) adds a scalar forget gate `α_t ∈ [0,1]` acting as weight decay on the fast weights.
- **KDA's innovation**: replaces GDN's coarse scalar decay with a **channel-wise (fine-grained) diagonal gate** `Diag(α_t)`, so each feature dimension maintains an independent forgetting rate — akin to Gated Linear Attention (GLA) but built on the delta rule rather than pure decay.
- KDA is a specialized variant of the general Diagonal-Plus-Low-Rank (DPLR) transition matrix family, but binds the two DPLR free variables (a, b) together, cutting second-level chunk matrix computations from four to two and eliminating three extra matmuls — roughly **2× the operator efficiency of general DPLR**.
- A bespoke hardware-efficient chunkwise algorithm (WY representation + UT transform) parallelizes the recurrence for training/inference throughput.
- No Position Encoding (NoPE) is used on the full-attention (MLA) layers — KDA becomes the primary position-aware operator (like short convolutions or sliding-window attention play in other hybrids), which also lets MLA collapse to efficient Multi-Query Attention at inference and simplifies long-context training (no RoPE frequency retuning/YaRN needed).

## Kimi Linear Architecture

- Backbone follows Moonlight; interleaves **3 KDA layers : 1 full MLA layer** (uniform 3:1 ratio) — found empirically optimal in ablations (vs. 0:1, 1:1, 7:1, 15:1).
- Layerwise hybridization (alternating whole layers) chosen over headwise mixing for simplicity/training stability.
- Q/K/V computed via ShortConv + Swish, with Q/K further L2-normalized for eigenvalue stability; per-channel decay via a low-rank projection; output uses a Sigmoid data-dependent gate + head-wise RMSNorm (mitigates attention sink, ablated to be better than swish gating or no gating).

## Results

- **Synthetic tasks** (palindrome, multi-query associative recall, stack/state-tracking): KDA achieves the highest accuracy and fastest convergence vs. GDN and Mamba2 as sequence length scales 256→2048; pure multiplicative-decay Mamba2 (no delta rule) fails all tasks.
- **Scaling**: at 1.4T training tokens, Kimi Linear is Pareto-optimal on MMLU-Pro (4k context) and RULER (128k context) vs. MLA and GDN-H, at 3.98× decoding acceleration.
- **Long context**: up to 6.3× faster TPOT than MLA at 1M-token decoding length (1.84ms vs. 11.48ms), while matching/exceeding MLA quality.
- KV cache usage reduced by up to 75% relative to full attention.
- Kernels (KDA chunkwise) and vLLM integration are open-sourced, along with pretrained/instruction-tuned checkpoints.
