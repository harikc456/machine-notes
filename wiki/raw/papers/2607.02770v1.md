# Gemma 4 Technical Report

**Authors:** Gemma Team, Google DeepMind
**Venue:** Technical Report, Jul 2026
**arXiv:** 2607.02770

## Summary

Gemma 4 is Google DeepMind's new generation of open-weight, natively multimodal LLMs (2.3B–31B params), spanning dense and MoE architectures, with a unified encoder-free variant for the 12B model that ingests raw audio/image patches directly. Introduces a "thinking mode" (reasoning trace before response), an MTP speculative-decoding drafter, QAT-quantized releases, and substantial long-context/KV-cache efficiency improvements. Gemma 4 31B is the top-ranked open **dense** model on Arena (Elo 1451), and the 26B-A4B MoE variant also ranks highly among open MoE models.

## Architectural Innovations

- **Thinking mode**: models emit a reasoning trace before the final answer, improving math/coding/reasoning benchmarks (following OpenAI o1-style approaches).
- **Long-context KV efficiency**: 5:1 (4:1 for the 2.3B model) ratio of local sliding-window to global full-attention layers; `p`-RoPE (p=0.25) on global layers, standard RoPE on local layers; **KV cache sharing** — values reused as keys in global attention layers (except E2B/E4B) — together reduce global KV cache footprint by up to 37.5%.
- **Encoder-free architecture (12B only)**: replaces separate 550M vision + 305M audio encoders with lightweight projection modules — raw 48×48×3 image patches (with 2D positional embeddings) and raw 40ms/16kHz audio chunks (640-dim vectors) are projected directly into the LLM embedding space, reducing memory fragmentation.
- **Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) drafter**: a small autoregressive 4-layer Transformer head trained for speculative decoding, cross-attending to the KV of the main model — eliminates the need for separate MTP prefill/drafting infrastructure. For E2B/E4B, the drafter's final projection is reduced from the full 262k vocabulary to a top-k cluster of 4096 tokens, cutting decode overhead while preserving acceptance rate.
- **Quantization-Aware Training (QAT)**: released in both mobile quantization (mixed int2/int4 weights + int8 activations) and Q4_0 (blockwise) formats. Vision encoder: 8-bit weights/activations → 2× memory reduction (400MB→200MB). Audio encoder: mixed {2,4,8}-bit weights + 8-bit activations → 78% on-disk footprint reduction (390MB→87MB in the 12B config) with 12–17% relative WER/BLEU improvement over Gemma 3n despite the smaller footprint.
- **Infrastructure**: TPUv5p/v6e training, ZeRO-3 optimizer sharding, Pathways multi-pod data-replica reduction, Slice-Granularity Elasticity (continues training through localized hardware failures with second-scale recovery instead of minutes).

## Results

- **Arena (blind human eval)**: Gemma 4 31B ranks #43 overall (Elo 1451), the top open **dense** model — ahead of Kimi K2.5 Thinking, Qwen 3.5 397B-A17B; 26B-A4B ranks #61 (Elo 1438).
- **Static benchmarks (31B vs. Gemma 3 27B)**: MMLU-Pro 85.2 vs. 67.6; AIME 2026 89.2 vs. 20.8; GPQA Diamond 84.3 vs. 42.4; Big Bench Extra Hard 74.4 vs. 19.3 — a broad leap across reasoning benchmarks.
- **Long context (Gemma 3 vs. Gemma 4, w/o thinking)**: RULER 128k accuracy 66.0→89.8 (26B-A4B)/96.4 (31B); LOFT Text Retrieval Recall@128k 8.6→66.3–79.5; GraphWalks F1 32.8→50.9–82.3 — a large jump in effective long-context capability.
- **Audio**: Gemma 4 E2B/E4B beat Gemma 3n of matching size despite dropping the dedicated audio encoder — 12%/10% relative CoVoST translation improvement, 17%/12% relative FLEURS ASR improvement, with 78% smaller on-disk audio footprint.
- **Vision**: E4B matches or beats Gemma 3 27B on all vision evals (MMMU Pro, MATH-Vision, InfographicVQA, OmniDocBench) despite far fewer parameters.
