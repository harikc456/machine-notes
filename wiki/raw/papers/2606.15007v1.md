# Nemotron 3 Ultra: Open, Efficient Mixture-of-Experts Hybrid Mamba-Transformer Model for Agentic Reasoning

NVIDIA, technical report, June 2026.

## Overview

550B total / 55B active parameter hybrid Mamba-Attention MoE model, the largest in the Nemotron 3
family (after Nemotron 3 Super). Pretrained on 20T text tokens, context-extended to 1M tokens,
post-trained with SFT + RLVR + Multi-teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD).

## Architecture

- **Layer pattern** (108 total layers, model dim 8192): repeating blocks of Mamba-2 layers
  interleaved with a smaller number of full Attention layers, with **LatentMoE** layers providing
  sparse scaling throughout (pattern shown as groups of Mamba-2 ×3, Attention, LatentMoE ×2/3/4
  repeating).
- 64 query heads, only 2 KV heads (aggressive GQA), head dim 128.
- Mamba-2: state dim 128, 8 groups, 256 heads, head dim 64.
- **LatentMoE** (Elango et al. 2026): 512 total experts per layer, top-22 activated, latent size
  2048, shared expert intermediate size 10240 — achieves better accuracy per active parameter than
  standard granular MoE.
- **Multi-Token Prediction (MTP)**: 2 MTP heads with *shared weights*, each a single attention layer
  + single MoE layer, used for robust autoregressive speculative drafting at inference.

## NVFP4 Pretraining

Same recipe as Nemotron 3 Super: E2M1 datatype, 2D block quantization on weights, Random Hadamard
Transforms on wgrad inputs, stochastic rounding on gradients. Final 15% of layers (16 layers),
Mamba output projections, latent projections, QKV/attention projections, MTP layers, and embeddings
kept in higher precision. Claimed largest-scale demonstration of stable NVFP4 training to date.
Ablations branching to BF16 at 5T/10T/16T-token checkpoints show <0.4% average relative train-loss
gap vs. full NVFP4 — smaller than the gap seen on smaller Nemotron 3 variants. Note: switching to
BF16 did *not* resolve a separate training-divergence issue observed later in training.

## Post-training

SFT → unified RLVR (multi-environment: reasoning, agentic, code, safety, usability, chat) → 10+
domain-specialized teacher models trained via targeted recipes → **MOPD** consolidates the teachers
into Ultra via dense token-level guidance on student-generated rollouts. Includes inference-time
reasoning-effort control (accuracy/compute trade-off knob).

## Results

Claims ~6× higher inference throughput than SOTA open LLMs (GLM-5.1-754B-A40B, Kimi-K2.6-1T-A32B,
Qwen-3.5-397B-17B) at 8K input / 64K output, at on-par accuracy — 5.9×, 4.8×, 1.6× respectively
against those three models. Throughput driven by the hybrid Mamba-Attention architecture (reduced
attention cost and KV cache footprint) combined with MoE's better accuracy-per-active-parameter.
Base, post-trained, and NVFP4-quantized checkpoints, training data, and recipes open-sourced on
HuggingFace.

## Relation to Existing Work

Extends [[deepseek-v4]]-style large hybrid MoE releases with a Mamba-Attention hybrid backbone
(vs. DeepSeek-V4's CSA/HCA hybrid attention) and NVFP4 pretraining at larger scale than prior
Nemotron reports. MTP head design (shared weights, single-attn + single-MoE layer) is a direct
speculative-decoding mechanism, relevant to [[speculative-decoding]].
