# VISReg: Variance-Invariance-Sketching Regularization for JEPA training

Wu, Balestriero, Levine (Altos Labs / Brown University), preprint, June 2026.

## Problem

Non-contrastive SSL/JEPA methods prevent embedding collapse either via heuristics (EMA
teacher-student, stop-gradient, frozen layers — DINO, BYOL) or explicit regularization.
Among the latter:
- **VICReg** decomposes the objective into variance + invariance + covariance terms. Covariance
  only captures second-order statistics — encourages decorrelation but doesn't enforce full
  distributional shape (can match mean/covariance yet be far from Gaussian).
- **SIGReg** (used in LeJEPA, see [[lejepa]]) sketches the embedding distribution toward an
  isotropic Gaussian via the Epps-Pulley test + Cramér-Wold theorem — enforces full distributional
  shape, but (1) its gradient vanishes as the embedding collapses, exactly when a corrective signal
  is needed most, and (2) it doesn't decouple scale from shape, limiting flexibility.

## Method

VISReg keeps VICReg's variance term (controls scale) but replaces the covariance term with a
**Sliced-Wasserstein-Distance sketching objective** that aligns the normalized embedding
distribution with an isotropic Gaussian along random 1D projections. This decouples scale and
shape into separate objectives, giving:
- distributional rigor beyond second-order statistics (like SIGReg),
- a gradient that stays robust even under collapse (unlike SIGReg),
- flexibility to reweight scale vs. shape per training regime (like VICReg).

## Results

- Pretrained on ImageNet-1K (ViT-B/16): SOTA OOD performance among compared methods; on-par with
  DINO on linear segmentation (dense prediction), though DINO retains ~3% higher in-domain linear
  probe accuracy — VISReg trades a little in-domain probe accuracy for materially better OOD
  transfer.
- Pretrained on ImageNet-22K (ViT-L/14): matches DINOv2's OOD performance despite DINOv2 using 10×
  more pretraining data (LVD-142M).
- Most robust to low-quality, long-tailed, and low-rank data regimes among VICReg/SIGReg/DINO;
  DINO struggles without careful hyperparameter tuning in these regimes.
- Scales linearly (no O(D²) covariance matrix, unlike VICReg/Barlow Twins).

## Relation to Existing Work

Directly extends the SIGReg line ([[lejepa]]) by fixing its two failure modes (vanishing gradient
under collapse, no scale/shape decoupling) while retaining VICReg's interpretable decomposed-loss
structure. Relevant to [[jepa]] as a further step in removing training heuristics from
joint-embedding architectures.
