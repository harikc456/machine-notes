# Large Language Diffusion Models (LLaDA)

**Authors:** Shen Nie, Fengqi Zhu, Zebin You, Xiaolu Zhang, Jingyang Ou, Jun Hu, Jun Zhou, Yankai Lin, Ji-Rong Wen, Chongxuan Li
**Affiliation:** Gaoling School of AI / Renmin University of China, Ant Group
**Venue:** NeurIPS 2025
**arXiv:** 2502.09992

## Summary

LLaDA is a diffusion language model trained **from scratch** (not adapted from an AR model) at genuine LLM scale — 8B parameters, 2.3T pretraining tokens — under the standard pretrain + SFT paradigm. It challenges the assumption that core LLM capabilities (scalability, in-context learning, instruction-following) are unique to autoregressive (AR) modeling, arguing these properties instead follow from the *generative modeling principles* (maximum likelihood / KL minimization), not the left-to-right factorization specifically.

## Method

- **Forward process**: masks tokens of x₀ independently with probability t ∈ [0,1] until fully masked at t=1.
- **Reverse process**: a Transformer **mask predictor** p_θ(·|x_t) (no causal mask — full bidirectional attention) predicts all masked tokens simultaneously, trained via a Monte Carlo estimate of the masked-token cross-entropy loss, which is a provable upper bound on the model's negative log-likelihood (unlike MaskGIT's heuristic objective, which lacks this maximum-likelihood link).
- **SFT**: same masking procedure, but only response tokens r₀ (given prompt p₀) are ever masked, training p_θ(r₀|p₀).
- **Inference**: reverse diffusion from a fully-masked response; at each step, remask a `s/t` fraction of predicted tokens (low-confidence remasking, à la Chang et al./MaskGIT) rather than pure random remasking. Supports flexible sampling — including AR-style and block-diffusion sampling — without retraining.
- Uses vanilla multi-head attention (not GQA) — LLaDA is **incompatible with KV caching** as a direct consequence of bidirectional, full-recompute-per-step attention.

## Results

- **Scalability**: matched-compute-budget comparisons (10²⁰–10²³ FLOPs) show LLaDA tracks or exceeds AR baselines on MMLU, ARC-C, CMMLU, PIQA, GSM8K, HumanEval — especially strong on math/Chinese-language tasks.
- **Benchmark comparison (8B Base)**: surpasses LLaMA2 7B Base on nearly all tasks; competitive overall with LLaMA3 8B Base (65.9 MMLU, 70.3 GSM8K, 35.4 HumanEval).
- **Instruction-tuned (SFT only, no RL)**: slightly behind LLaMA3 8B Instruct (which has RL alignment), but demonstrates strong instruction-following, multi-turn dialogue, and translation with only SFT.
- **Reversal reasoning**: on a poem-completion reversal task (496 Chinese poem pairs), LLaDA shows consistent forward/reversal performance and **beats GPT-4o on the reversal task**, directly addressing the "reversal curse" that afflicts AR models — attributed to LLaDA's non-causal, multi-directional training objective.
- Compute cost: Nie et al.'s earlier finding that masked diffusion needs ~16× more compute than AR to match likelihood; this paper argues likelihood is an indirect proxy and extends scaling to 10²⁰–10²³ FLOPs where downstream-task competitiveness holds.

## Relevance to Wiki

The **foundational, full-scale (8B, from-scratch) entry** in the [[diffusion-language-models]] landscape — a precursor/sibling to [[block-diffusion]] (BD3-LM, restores KV caching via AR-over-blocks) and [[i-dlm]] (introspective consistency, causal-attention DLM built by *converting* an AR model rather than training from scratch). LLaDA is the "pure DLM trained from scratch at scale" reference point the field measures against (e.g., I-DLM benchmarks against LLaDA-2.1-mini).
