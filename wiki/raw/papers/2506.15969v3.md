# LazyEviction: Lagged KV Eviction with Attention Pattern Observation for Efficient Long Reasoning

**Authors**: Haoyue Zhang, Hualei Zhang, Xiaosong Ma, Jie Zhang, Song Guo
**Affiliation**: HKUST, HK PolyU
**arXiv**: 2506.15969, Oct 2025

## Core Problem

Existing KV cache eviction methods (H₂O, TOVA, Scissorhands) perform greedy per-step evictions using current or accumulated attention scores. On long reasoning tasks (Chain-of-Thought, MATH500, GSM8K), these methods fail because they miss the **Token Importance Recurrence (TIR)** phenomenon.

## Key Observation: Token Importance Recurrence (TIR)

>95% of tokens in reasoning tasks exhibit recurrent attention patterns: a token receives high attention at step t, then low attention for an interval, then regains high attention at a later step. Examples:
- Initial problem conditions (referenced repeatedly across reasoning steps)
- Intermediate conclusions (reactivated when later steps need them)
- Boundary/summary tokens

Existing greedy methods evict tokens during their low-attention interval — catastrophically losing them before they can be reused. On GSM8K at 50% KV budget: TOVA −20%, H₂O −20% relative score vs. full cache.

## Method: Observation Window-Based Lagged Eviction

LazyEviction uses two components:

**Recurrence Interval Tracking**: For each token i, track its Maximum Recurrence Interval (MRI) — the longest observed gap between consecutive high-attention activations. When token i receives attention > threshold α, update its timestamp TS_i. MRI = max(MRI_{t-1}, TS_t − TS_{t-1}).

**MRI-Centric Eviction Policy**: Execute KV eviction at intervals of W steps (observation window) rather than every step. At eviction time, compute importance score from:
- H1-score: 2σ(−(t − TS[i]) / MRI[i]) — likelihood token is still in recurrence interval
- H2-score: 2σ(−1 / MRI[i]) — tokens with smaller MRI (more frequent) are more critical

Tokens with (t − TS[i]) > MRI[i] are predicted to be done with recurrence and safe to evict.

## Results

- 50–70% KV cache reduction while maintaining comparable accuracy on reasoning tasks
- Consistently outperforms H₂O and TOVA on MATH500 and GSM8K under same compression ratio
- Verified on 4B–32B reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama series)
- 80th percentile MRI < 175 tokens for Qwen on MATH500 — observation window of this size captures most recurrences

## vs. Prior Eviction Methods

| Method | Strategy | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| StreamingLLM | Keep initial + recent | Misses recurring mid-sequence tokens |
| TOVA | Current attention | Myopic; evicts during low-attention intervals |
| H₂O, Scissorhands | Accumulated attention | Still misses future recurrences |
| **LazyEviction** | MRI tracking + lagged window | Retains tokens until MRI exceeded |
