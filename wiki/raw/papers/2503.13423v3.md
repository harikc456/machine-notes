# SuperBPE: Space Travel for Language Models

**Alisa Liu\*, Jonathan Hayase\*, Valentin Hofmann, Sewoong Oh, Noah A. Smith, Yejin Choi**
UW / NVIDIA / Allen Institute for AI — COLM 2025 — arXiv:2503.13423

## Core Contribution

Introduces **superword tokenization**: extends BPE to produce tokens that span multiple whitespace-delimited words (multi-word expressions). Achieves this via a **pretokenization curriculum**:

- **Stage 1** (vocabulary slots 0→t): standard BPE with whitespace pretokenization — learn subword tokens
- **Stage 2** (slots t→T): lift whitespace constraint — BPE merges now freely cross word boundaries, learning superword tokens
- Transition point `t` is the key hyperparameter (best: t = 180k for T = 200k)

No changes to model architecture, training framework, or decoding strategy.

## Key Results (8B models, 200k vocab, ~330B tokens, OLMo2 setup)

| Metric | BPE | SuperBPE (t=180k) |
|---|---|---|
| Bytes/token (encoding efficiency) | 4.45 | 6.63 |
| Avg downstream (30 tasks) | 39.8 | 43.8 (+4.0%) |
| MMLU | 36.5 | 44.7 (+8.2%) |
| Wins on individual tasks | — | 25/30 |
| Inference FLOPs/byte | 3.75×10⁹ | 2.54×10⁹ (−32%) |

## Why It Works

SuperBPE tokens often capture **semantically vacuous multi-word expressions** (e.g., *by the way*, *of course*, *in the long run*) that are fixed, context-independent, and require memorization. Under BPE, these generate many low-loss, easy-to-predict tokens. Under SuperBPE, they become single tokens — eliminating the easy predictions. This shifts compute from trivial tokens to hard ones, producing more **uniform per-token difficulty** (narrower loss distribution: fewer very-high and very-low loss tokens).

SuperBPE's encoding efficiency scales better with vocabulary size than BPE: BPE saturates at ~50k vocab (bounded by whitespace-delimited words), while SuperBPE continues improving as it discovers common word sequences.

## Scaling

- Advantage consistent across 680M, 1.9B, 8B model sizes
- SuperBPE matching inference compute consistently achieves lower BPB at all scales
- BPB is not a reliable ranking signal across tokenizers (SuperBPE can have higher average BPB yet better downstream task performance — because its hard-token distribution differs)
